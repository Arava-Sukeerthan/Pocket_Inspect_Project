## 8. Evidence and search limitations

- Search scope: one search batch (2026-10-01), OpenAlex primary with Crossref verification, publication date >= 2016, top 15 (broad) or top 12 (targeted) results screened per query, plus 11 known-item lookups. No IEEE Xplore, Scopus or Web of Science search and no citation snowballing. Absence from the corpus is not absence from the literature.
- Full-text coverage: 10 of 54 records are full-text verified (P002, P011, P013, P015, P016, P020, P029, P031, P033, P034). 8 queued records stayed blocked: P001, P007, P017 and P032 inaccessible; P018, P019, P022 and P023 abstract only. The other 36 records are coded from abstracts.
- Unknown dominates: for every characteristic, 29-38 of the 46 core primary records are Unknown. Unknown is never counted as No.
- Version limitations: P029, P031 and P033 were read from same-version camera-ready / journal-layout copies; the ACM PDFs were not compared. The P011 Fig. 4 labels were read in arXiv v2 only.
- P013 Tables 4-5 are visually unverified and P013 accuracy_metrics is blank. This is an unresolved evidence field. It is not evidence of poor accuracy, missing evaluation or a gap. P013 is peripheral/contextual and not counted as core evidence.
- Publisher sites (ACM DL, ScienceDirect, doi.org and others) are blocked by this environment's network policy. Access limits are not evidence of a gap.
- The visual_inspection, three_d_print_inspection and review_records assignments are analysis-only auditor proposals pending researcher review. The audit A-E relevance classes were not used, because they were never approved.
